[2026-04-16 20:xx] Root cause analysis: "TX=1023" origin trace
- r8125 upstream-like driver defaults to 1024 descriptors for both core rings:
  - data-eth-src/drivers/r8125/src/r8125.h: NUM_TX_DESC=1024, NUM_RX_DESC=1024
  - data-eth-src/drivers/r8125/src/r8125_n.c: rtl8125_set_ring_size(..., NUM_RX_DESC, NUM_TX_DESC)
- No 1023 ring-size constant appears in r8125/r8125_ioss path; 1023 appears in IOSS DT/fallback layer:
  - data-eth-src/drivers/ioss/ioss_of.c: IOSS_R8125_RX_RING_SIZE=1023, TX_RING_SIZE=1024
  - pcie_enabler variants inject qcom,ring-size with RX=1023 and TX=1024.
- IPA dir numeric mapping is: TX=0, RX=1 (include/linux/ipa_eth.h).
- In preflight logs, the non-power-of-two ring (32736 = 1023*32) is on dir=1, i.e. RX, not TX.
- Therefore observed "TX=1023" is a direction-label interpretation issue; actual configured 1023 ring in this stack is RX-side policy from IOSS/DT injection, not natural r8125 upstream behavior.
